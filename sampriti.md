**ABOUT SAMPRITI**

**Rooted in India's living herbal lineages.**

India's medical heritage extends across many systems of plant knowledge that evolved through geography, ecology, and lived experience. Alongside Siddha medicine of the South, there exist Himalayan healing traditions, village pharmacopeias, and tribal systems that have preserved sophisticated botanical understanding for centuries.

These include **Sowa-Rigpa** of the trans-Himalayan regions, **Vaidya paramparas** of central India, and tribal medical traditions such as **Irula**, **Kani**, **Gond**, and **Toda** ethnomedicine - each shaped by forests, mountains, coastlines, and climate.

Sampriti arises from this wider continuum.

Over the years, I have travelled across India - through forests, hills, river belts, temples, homes, and healing spaces - learning directly from landscapes and people who live in close relationship with plants. What drew me deeply was the way herbs were understood as living forces: their temperament, compatibility, timing, and restraint in use.

Sampriti reflects this way of knowing - careful, experiential, and precise.

**OUR PHILOSOPHY**

**Plants shaped by land, time, and rhythm.**

Across traditional cultures, herbal knowledge has always been inseparable from ecology. Plants were observed through seasons of growth, dormancy, flowering, and decay - and harvested with attention to soil health, climate, and timing.

Ancient agricultural societies recognised that potency is influenced by where a plant grows, how it is harvested, and how gently it is handled. Forest-grown plants, mountain herbs, and wild-harvested botanicals were especially valued for their resilience and complexity.

At Sampriti, we source as much as possible from **wild, forested, mountainous, and non-industrial landscapes**, working with small cultivators, gatherers, and suppliers who respect natural cycles and regeneration.

Formulation begins with listening - to the plant, the land, and the balance required in the body.
