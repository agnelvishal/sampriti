About Sampriti

India’s healing heritage is woven from diverse plant traditions—Siddha, Himalayan, village, and tribal - rooted in land and lived ritual. Beyond Ayurveda, traditions like Sowa Rigpa and the wisdom of Irula, Kani, Gond, and Toda communities preserve centuries of embodied botanical knowledge.

Sampriti emerges from within this continuum.

We create ritual formulations inspired by India’s diverse herbal lineages, crafted in small batches with intentional sourcing, precise dosages, and uncompromising purity. Each blend is designed to support daily vitality, deep restoration, and long-term resilience—moving from health toward a state of luminous balance.

Our Philosophy

Across traditional cultures, herbal knowledge has always been inseparable from ecology. Plants were observed through seasons of growth, dormancy, flowering, and decay - and harvested with attention to soil health, climate, and timing.

At Sampriti, we source as much as possible from wild, forested, mountainous, and non-industrial landscapes, working with small cultivators, gatherers, and suppliers who respect natural cycles and regeneration.

Every formulation is created without additives, fillers, or artificial preservatives, allowing the innate intelligence of each plant to remain intact.
